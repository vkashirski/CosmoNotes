COSMONOTES - how to build and install
=====================================

1. Build the installer
   - Double-click  build.bat
   - Wait until it says "Done!".
   - A folder opens with "CosmoNotes Setup 1.1.1.exe".
   (Node.js must be installed - you already did this for the first version.)

2. Install
   - Double-click "CosmoNotes Setup 1.1.1.exe".
   - If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": click "More info",
     then "Run anyway".
   - It replaces the old "Sticky Pins" app automatically. Your notes come
     with you: the first time CosmoNotes opens it copies them over from
     the old Sticky Pins folder (the old folder is left untouched as a
     safety copy).

Other laptops
   Copy "CosmoNotes Setup 1.1.1.exe" over and run it. Nothing else needed.


WHERE ARE MY NOTES SAVED?
   Only on this computer, in:  %APPDATA%\CosmoNotes
   (paste that into the File Explorer address bar to open it)
   - notes        one small file per note, saved the moment you type
   - attachments  your photos, drawings and voice recordings
   - backups      a full copy of your notes from each of the last 30 days
   - groups.json  your groups and their names

   To move ALL your notes to another laptop: quit CosmoNotes on both
   (right-click the note icon near the clock > Quit), then copy the whole
   "CosmoNotes" folder above to the same place on the other laptop.

   Uninstalling the app does NOT delete your notes.


LOCKED NOTES
   The lock hides a note's contents behind your password or PIN, in the
   note itself and in the notes list. It's a privacy screen, not
   encryption: the note is still stored as a normal file on this computer.

   Forgot the password? Quit CosmoNotes, open %APPDATA%\CosmoNotes,
   open settings.json in Notepad, change the "lock" line to
       "lock": null,
   save, and start CosmoNotes again. Your locked notes stay locked; choose a
   new password the next time you lock a note (or in Settings) and they
   open with that one.


QUICK TIPS
   Top bar:    [grid] all notes   [+] new note   [pin] stay on top
               [^] roll up / unroll   [...] title, colour, lock, dates
   Bottom bar: B I U S, bullets, checklist, [A] text colour/highlight/size,
               spelling on/off, text layout lock, draw over, [+] photos,
               voice, sketches, links, tables and more.
   - Click a photo, then drag the round handle at its corner to resize it.
   - The squiggle button (next to +) lets you draw on top of the note: pen,
     highlighter, lines, arrows, squares and circles, over or behind the text.
     Hold Shift for straight lines and perfect shapes. Esc or Done to stop.
   - The button next to spelling locks the text layout: text and pictures keep
     their width and you scroll sideways if the note gets narrower.
   - In the notes list, drag a note onto another note to make a group.
     Drop notes or whole groups onto a group to put them inside (groups
     can sit inside groups, like folders). Click a group to open it; the
     palette button there sets its look (stack, folder, envelope,
     notebook), its colour, and can recolour every note inside at once.
   - Right-click a note or group in the list to lock it, move it, ungroup
     it or put it in the Bin, without opening it.
   - The bin button asks before deleting. Shift+click it to skip that.
   - The sort button next to search: last edited / date created,
     newest or oldest first, groups first or mixed in.
   - Spelling is ignored by default. Turn it on for all notes in
     Settings, or per note with its spelling button.
   - Shortcuts: Ctrl+N new note, Ctrl+B/I/U bold/italic/underline,
     Ctrl+T strikethrough, Ctrl+Shift+L bullets, Ctrl+Shift+C checklist,
     Ctrl+K link, Ctrl+W close note.
